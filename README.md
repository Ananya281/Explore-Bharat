# 🌏 **Explore Bharat**  
**Culture and Heritage Website**

---

## 🎯 **Project Overview**

Explore Bharat is an innovative web platform designed to showcase India's vibrant and diverse cultural heritage. The platform allows users to dive into the richness of India's traditions, monuments, festivals, and artifacts through interactive and personalized experiences. With the integration of AI/ML technologies, Explore Bharat aims to revolutionize cultural exploration, making it engaging and educational for users worldwide.

---

## 🛠️ **Features**

- 🌍 **Region-Specific Exploration:**  
  Discover the cultural diversity of Indian states with detailed information on history, traditions, festivals, and cuisine.

- 🏛️ **Image Recognition:**  
  Upload images of monuments, temples, or artifacts to receive instant historical and cultural background.

- 🗺️ **Interactive Maps:**  
  Region-wise interactive maps highlighting key cultural landmarks and attractions.

- 🎭 **Festivals and Traditions Section:**  
  Explore the vibrant festivals and unique traditions of each state.

- 📊 **Data Protection:**  
  Robust security measures to ensure user data privacy.

---

## 🚀 **Technologies Used**

- **Frontend:** React.js, TailwindCSS, JavaScript  
- **Backend:** Flask, Node.js  
- **Database:** MongoDB  
- **AI/ML Integration:** TensorFlow, OpenCV, FastAPI, Pandas  
- **Deployment:** Vercel, Render  

---

## 🎯 **How It Works**

1. Users can search for cultural and historical information by exploring the state-wise details.
2. An AI-powered image recognition feature identifies cultural artifacts, monuments, or temples from uploaded images.
3. Interactive maps to enhance the user experience.
4. Cultural insights are enriched with region-specific festivals, cuisines, and traditions.

---

## 🎯 **Target Audience**

- Individuals aged 15-65 with a keen interest in India's cultural heritage.
- Educational institutions, cultural organizations, and tourism boards.
